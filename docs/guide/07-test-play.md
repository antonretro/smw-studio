# Test Play

Press **▶ Play** (top right) to play the level you're editing, right inside SMW Studio. It includes your unsaved changes and doesn't change your ROM file.

## Controls

- Keyboard: arrows to move, `X` jump, `Z` run / pick up, `C` spin jump, `S` (X button), `Enter` start.
- A game controller works too.
- `R` restarts the level, `Esc` stops playing.

When Mario dies or finishes, the level starts again straight away.

## Play options (the ▾ next to Play)

- **Infinite lives**, **Invincible** (falling into pits still counts), **Infinite time**.
- **Power-up**: start small, big, with a cape or with a fire flower.
- **Start at the midway point**: start as if the midway tape was already touched.
