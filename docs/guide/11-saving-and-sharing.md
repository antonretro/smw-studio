# Saving and sharing

## Saving

- **Save** (`Ctrl+S`) writes your changes into the ROM file.
- **File → Save a copy as…** writes a separate ROM with your changes. The open ROM stays as it is.
- **File → Show ROM in folder** opens the folder with your ROM, its backups and its AddmusicK folder.

## Backups

- `<name>.sfc.bak`: your ROM as it was the first time you saved it in SMW Studio.
- **SMW Studio Backups** folder: a copy from before every Toolbox import and music insert.

To go back, copy a backup over your ROM (keep the ROM's name).

## Lunar Magic

SMW Studio saves in the same format as Lunar Magic, so you can open your hack in either one. It works out Lunar Magic's settings (entrance formats, per-screen exit flags, custom backgrounds) from the ROM itself.

## Sharing your hack

ROM files can't be shared: they contain Nintendo's game. Hacks are shared as **patches** (`.bps`) that people apply to their own clean ROM. Making patches from inside SMW Studio is planned. Until then, tools such as Floating IPS (Flips) make a `.bps` from your clean ROM and your hack.
