# Level settings

**Properties → Level settings** has every option of the level in plain words. Changes show in the editor right away.

- **Music**: the song. The list has the level music choices, **Your songs ★** (songs you added) and **All the game's songs**.
- **Tileset**: the ground and block graphics (Normal, Castle, Underground, Ghost House...).
- **Enemy graphics**: which group of enemies shows correctly.
- **Background colour** and **Background palette**: the colours behind everything.
- **Ground palette** and **Enemy palette**: colours of blocks and of enemies.
- **Time limit**: none, 200, 300 or 400.
- **Vertical scroll**: when the camera moves up and down.
- **Item memory**: which collected coins and blocks stay collected.
- **Layer 3 in front**: water and tide layers drawn over everything.

## Level length and size

**Properties → Level → Length (screens)** with **−** / **+**. Making a level longer adds empty screens at the end.
